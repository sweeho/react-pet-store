# Catalog Browsing Capability

## Executive Summary

Customers need to browse and search the product catalog organized by categories, view product details with pricing and attributes, and see results formatted for their preferred language. This capability provides category listing, product retrieval, and paginated search results.

## Scope

- Category management and retrieval with localization
- Product listing and search with pagination
- Item details with pricing and attributes
- Multi-locale support (en_US, ja_JP, zh_CN)
- Pagination metadata for UI navigation
